Turn typed text into handwritten pages

Paste your notes, answers or a letter. Get A4 pages that look written with a real pen and photographed on a phone. Everything runs in your browser, so your text stays on your computer.

How it works

  1. Write. Type or paste text. Headings, bold, lists, tables, highlights and colours work with simple markdown.
  2. Pick the hand. Choose from 21 handwriting styles, print or cursive, or load your own font.
  3. Pick the pens and paper. Blue body text, black headings, pencil tables. Plain, ruled, grid or aged paper.
  4. Pick the camera. Clean scan, scanner app, close-up phone shot, flash, dim room or photocopy.
  5. Download. One PDF, or a PNG for each page.

Why it looks real

  • Every letter outline is bent a little, so no two letters match.
  • Lines drift, words wander off the baseline, and ink pressure changes.
  • The writing gets a little sloppier down the page.
  • Pens have their own grain and bleed. Pencil looks like graphite.
  • Camera looks add keystone, paper bow, glare, soft focus and grain.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign up and no limit. The code is open source under the MIT licence.

Does my text leave my computer?

No. The pages are drawn in your browser. Your text is not uploaded anywhere.

Why does every letter look a little different?

Each letter outline is bent slightly, and its size, angle, spacing and ink pressure change. No two letters on a page are the same.

Can I use different colours for different parts?

Yes. The Ink tab sets a pen for headings, bold text, tables and quotes. For one phrase, select it and press a colour dot, or type {red}text{/}.

Can I use my own handwriting font?

Yes. On the Hand tab, load a .ttf, .otf or .woff2 file.
